Registration Instructions

Steps to Register

  1. Enter your Register Number.
  2. Enter your full name.
  3. Provide a valid university email.
  4. Select your department and semester.
  5. Select one or more courses.
  6. Click the Register button.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Student must have a valid Register Number.
  • Student must use a valid email address.
  • Semester must be between 1 and 8.
  • Selected courses must be available.
  • All mandatory fields must be completed.

Visit the University Website for additional information.
